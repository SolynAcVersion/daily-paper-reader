## 摘要
近期工作通过预训练 3D 模型的几何特征来增强视觉语言模型，期望几何信号能够提升空间推理。然而，我们发现，仅将几何特征融合并在标准空间问答上训练，对高层多跳任务只能带来边际改进。我们将这一差距归因于训练信号问题：标准空间问答很大程度上可以仅凭视觉特征和语言先验回答，因此几何通路获得弱梯度，无法与视觉特征有效整合。为了提供一种需要几何信息的训练信号，我们提出新视角语义渲染作为辅助训练任务，要求模型预测未观测视角的语义布局，这受人类在空间推理中在脑内模拟新视角能力的启发。该任务鼓励两条通路的联合使用：几何提供依赖位姿的可见性，而视觉提供语义内容。我们的辅助任务在三个基准上均较几何增强基线取得一致提升（在 VSI-Bench 上最高 +1.6，在 ReVSI 上 +2.2，在我们的 3D-Point-QA 数据集上 +2.9），并且我们的完整模型在 VSI-Bench 和 ReVSI 上超越了此前的开源方法。项目页面：https://yuqunw.github.io/Render2Reason/。

## Abstract
Recent works augment Vision-Language Models with geometry features from pretrained 3D models, expecting that the geometric signal will boost spatial reasoning. However, we find that simply fusing geometry features and training on standard spatial QA yields only marginal improvements on high-level multi-hop tasks. We attribute this gap to a training-signal problem: standard spatial QA can be largely answered from visual features and language priors, so the geometry pathway receives weak gradients and fails to integrate with the visual features. To provide a training signal that requires geometry, we propose \textbf{novel-view semantic rendering} as an auxiliary training task that requires the model to predict the semantic layout of an unobserved viewpoint, inspired by humans' ability to mentally simulate novel viewpoints during spatial reasoning. This task encourages joint use of both pathways: geometry provides pose-dependent visibility, while vision provides semantic content. Our auxiliary task yields consistent improvements over the geometry-augmented baseline across all three benchmarks (up to +1.6 on VSI-Bench, +2.2 on ReVSI, +2.9 on our 3D-Point-QA dataset) and our full model surpasses prior open-source methods on VSI-Bench and on ReVSI. Project page: https://yuqunw.github.io/Render2Reason/.
